# Raise the Bar: references

The support behind each rule of the manifesto `plugin/manifestos/raise-the-bar.md`, for anyone who checks a rule. A writer applying the manifesto never needs this file.

## Limits

No study tests a manifesto-style text on performance; the rules assemble adjacent evidence. Most findings come from feedback to one person, often a student or a child. The largest effects appeared in readers who distrusted the writer or had fallen behind, and readers who already perform well and trust the writer should expect small gains. Durability is the least-tested area, and the model evidence measures accuracy and compliance, not persistence over long tasks. The rules from Locke & Latham, Seijts et al., Grant & Hofmann, Oyserman et al., Cialdini et al., Oppenheimer, Kluger & DeNisi, Eden et al., McNatt & Judge, Bandura, Binsch et al., Meyer et al., and Rains carry over from earlier reading without a fresh check of their sources. The verification behind this file stays outside the repository.
