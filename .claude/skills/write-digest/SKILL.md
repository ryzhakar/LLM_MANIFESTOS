---
name: write-digest
description: "Write one manifesto's digest: the plain text a small model carries in place of the full manifesto. \"write the digest\", \"digest this manifesto\", \"cut the digest\", \"re-cut the digest\", or a manifesto under manifestos/ with no digest or a digest older than its version."
---

- Read the manifesto whole. Bind to it (the `manifesto-oath` skill).
- List every distinct demand it makes. Collapse restatements. Discard stories, figures, names, the closing choice.
- Write each demand as one plain, complete sentence a reader can obey without the original. No metaphor the original explains, no idiom, no ellipsis.
- Keep the manifesto's own order: tagline, checklist, headings, body.
- Fold each boundary into the sentence it limits.
- Add one line that applies the rules to every reply, including the drift rule if the manifesto has one.
- Apply Strunk (`instructions/strunk_spr_v4_compressed.xml`): active voice, positive form, concrete words, parallel lists, key words at the end, no needless words.
- Check against the full text: every demand present or deliberately dropped, nothing imported.
- No header, no frontmatter, no title. Portable markdown. Under 350 words.
- Save as `digests/<slug>.md`. Done.
