---
name: write-digest
description: "Write the first cut of one manifesto's digest: the plain text a small model carries in place of the full manifesto. \"write the digest\", \"digest this manifesto\", \"cut the digest\", or a manifesto under manifestos/ with no digest or a digest older than its version."
---

<read-and-bind>
Read the manifesto whole; never read a summary of it.
Bind to it through the `manifesto-oath` skill; never write unbound.
</read-and-bind>

<list-the-demands>
List every distinct demand, one thing the manifesto tells its reader to do or refuse; never list one twice.
Collapse restatements into one demand; never carry a restatement as a new demand.
Discard stories, figures, names, and the closing choice; never carry one into the digest, the plain text a small model carries in place of the manifesto.
</list-the-demands>

<save-the-demand-list>
Save the demand list as the demands record through the `write-eval-record` skill; never hold it in the conversation alone.
</save-the-demand-list>

<write-the-sentences>
Write each demand as one plain, complete sentence a reader can obey without the original; never write one that needs the original.
Write every sentence in literal, full words; never use a metaphor the original explains, an idiom, or an ellipsis.
</write-the-sentences>

<keep-the-order>
Keep the manifesto's own order, tagline, checklist, headings, body; never reorder by your own priority.
</keep-the-order>

<fold-the-boundaries>
Fold each boundary, the place where a demand stops applying, into the sentence it limits; never write a boundary as its own sentence.
</fold-the-boundaries>

<add-the-standing-line>
Add one line that applies the rules to every reply, including the drift rule, the rule that late replies get the same check as early ones, where the manifesto has one; never leave the digest without it.
</add-the-standing-line>

<apply-strunk>
Apply Strunk's rules, active voice, positive form, concrete words, parallel lists, key words at the end, no needless words; never deliver a sentence that breaks one.
</apply-strunk>

<check-against-the-source>
Check the digest against the full text, every demand present or deliberately dropped and nothing imported; never check against memory of the text.
</check-against-the-source>

<shape-the-file>
Write the body alone, in portable markdown, markdown with no feature past paragraphs and lists, under 350 words; never add a header, a frontmatter, or a title.
</shape-the-file>

<save-the-file>
Save the digest as `digests/<slug>.md`, where `slug` is the manifesto's file name without its extension; never save it elsewhere.
</save-the-file>
