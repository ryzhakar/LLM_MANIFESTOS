## What the series showed, in the process's terms

1. Check resolution before any recut. Mean violations per reply, test split: digest 6.50, 8.50, 7.08 over cuts 1 to 3 (cut 2 against cut 1 +2.00 [-0.50, 4.83]; cut 3 against cut 2 -1.42 [-4.00, 1.50]); digest minus terse -1.50 [-3.75, 0.58], +0.08 [-2.17, 2.50], -0.83 [-3.08, 1.67]. The same baseline conditions, judged again in each cut, moved by 0.50 to 2.75 violations per reply on the test split (nothing 11.92, 12.92, 12.08; full 17.42, 20.17, 19.17; placebo 9.67, 10.92, 10.67; terse 8.00, 8.42, 7.92); the series handoff calls the judge noise "1 to 3" (HANDOFF.md). No record separates an edit's effect from this noise: each edit was expected to move the digest by about -0.3 per reply (petro-cut-2.md and petro-cut-3.md, line 251) and the observed moves were +2.00 and -1.42. Proposal: before diagnose-and-recut, read the last cut's interval half-width; when it exceeds the effect one change is expected to have, raise repeats or enlarge the unit of change instead of recutting.
2. Separate "no effect" from "cannot see". The stop rule fired when two cuts in a row had `changed_vs_previous` false (cuts 2 and 3); with the intervals above that outcome cannot tell an edit that did nothing from an instrument that cannot resolve it. Proposal: three stop outcomes in the record: passed; stopped, no detectable effect (the interval excludes the effect of interest); inconclusive (interval too wide: more repeats, or stop and say so).
3. Freeze gates and controls from a measured noise estimate. The gate "beats terse" asks for a gap the instrument cannot show at one repeat (intervals 4.3 to 4.8 wide). The check named "near full" (digest minus full at or below a margin; tools/digest-eval/digest_eval/gate.py, line 26) is no anchor for a small reader: on the test split the 11 manifestos joined scored 17.4, 20.2 and 19.2 against 11.9, 12.9 and 12.1 for nothing loaded, and on all tasks 14.35, 16.22 and 15.44 against 12.96, 13.22 and 12.30, but on the dev split the order reverses (11.00, 11.91 and 11.36 against 14.09, 13.54 and 12.54). The terse control carried domain words ("Answer briefly and precisely, and write careful, minimal, well-typed Python and Rust."), so "beats terse" measured the digest's marginal value over a domain aim. Proposal: spec freeze runs a noise pilot (same tasks twice, nothing changed), writes its estimate into the spec and derives the gate gap and the repeats from it; the spec names what the terse control contains; every comparison with the full text is reported per split, dev and test apart.
4. Judge scale drifts between waves. Baselines judged again in each cut moved (figures in item 1). Borrowed baselines changed packet composition: the digest condition's test mean read 11.17 in cut 2's discarded first pass, judged in single-reply packets, against 6.50 in cut 1, with 6 of 23 replies marked not done against 0 (cut-2/judge-solo/README.md under the series directory; events.md, receipt of 2026-10-13 about 09:56 UTC). Proposal: one fixed anchor set (planted-violation items) inside every judging wave and a rescale from it, or judge every condition of a cut in the same packets; compare no numbers across waves without anchors. Kept as is: one judge configuration per series.
5. Score completion by reply type, and show script and judge side by side. In cut 3 two digest replies on test tasks (ambig-parse, lines-upgrade) were a bare probe (`ls -la`, `cat Cargo.toml | grep -A5 serde`) and were counted not done by the script; a third (ambig-sort, a dev task outside the gate) was a bare `ls -la` too, the script counted it done and the judge did not. Completion 0.833 is 10 of 12 test pairs against the 0.9 gate; cut 2 scored 0.917 and passed, and its same-task replies were an answer, a one-line probe with a statement, and a bare listing on a dev task. The gate reads the script's verdict first (tools/digest-eval/digest_eval/score.py, line 90); the judge marked `done` false on 8 of 23 digest singles in cut 3, 8 in cut 2 and 2 in cut 1, against 2, 1 and 0 by script, so most of the apparent completion gap is script against judge. The reader had no tools. Proposal: completion recorded as answer, probe, refusal or empty, with both verdicts side by side and every disagreement listed, so a drop is attributable.
